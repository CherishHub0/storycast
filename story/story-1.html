The Day the Power Went Out

Video · 30 seconds · Captions and transcript included

Transcript

So last month, the night before a big deadline I had, the power just went off.

The whole street, was completely dark.

My laptop was on nine percent and I hadn't saved anything. Nothing.

I sat there like, okay, that's it, I'm done.

Then my neighbour knocked with a candle and a power bank.

I finished at two in the morning. We barely even talked. I still owe her a piece of cake till today.
