About StoryCast

StoryCast shares short audio and video stories so that everyone gets the same experience, whether they can hear them or not.

Our mission

Too many videos and recordings only work if you can hear them. We think a story should work for everyone. So every story on StoryCast comes with captions and a full written transcript, right on the same page.

StoryCast is a student project. It is built mainly for deaf and hard-of-hearing people, and it also helps anyone watching with the sound off, on a noisy bus, or in a second language.

Accessibility features

The site is built to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. Here is what that means in practice:

  • Captions. Every video has a captions file you can switch on in the player.
  • Transcripts. A full written version of every story sits under the player. You can hide or show it.
  • Keyboard friendly. Every link and button can be reached with the Tab key.
  • Skip link. Press Tab once on any page to jump straight to the main content.
  • Clear focus. A thick amber outline shows where you are on the page.
  • Strong contrast. All text colours have a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 against their background.
  • Readable font. We use Atkinson Hyperlegible, a font designed to be easy to tell letters apart.
  • Works when zoomed. The layout adjusts to small screens and larger text.
  • No surprises. Nothing plays by itself.

How to use StoryCast

  1. Go to the Home page and pick a story.
  2. Press play, then use the CC button in the video player to turn captions on or off.
  3. Prefer to read? Open the transcript under the video.
  4. Finish by checking the related stories on the side of the page.

Found something that does not work for you? Tell us on GitHub and we will fix it.
